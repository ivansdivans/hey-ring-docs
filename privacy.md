# Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

"Hey Ring" is a personal app for viewing the developer's own Oura data.

## Data access and storage

The app accesses all sort of data authorized through Oura.
Authentication takes place through Oura.
The app does not collect or store your Oura password.

The access token is stored in the iOS Keychain.
Downloaded health data is stored locally on the device.
The app has no developer-operated backend, advertising, or analytics,
and does not send health data to the developer or other third parties.

## Disconnecting and deleting data

Use "Disconnect and delete data" to remove the saved access token
and all locally cached Oura data from the device.

The app stops accessing Oura until you sign in again.
This action does not revoke the token at Oura or delete
data from your Oura account.
